# Artwork sources

The icon uses the project's original history-arrow symbol. The flat editable version is `icon.svg`. The production raster is `icon-glass-master.png`; the extension uses PNG exports at 16, 32, 48, and 128 pixels. `mesh-backdrop.png` is used only in store graphics. Neither master raster is loaded by the extension.

The two master rasters were generated with the built-in image generation tool. No external API key was used or stored. Browser rendering composes the screenshot typography and actual UI captures and exports the required sizes. It does not generate or alter song metadata or cover artwork.

## Icon prompt

Use case: style-transfer. Asset: final production Chrome extension app icon, square, genuinely transparent background. Edit target: supplied icon. Preserve its original symbol: two short straight horizontal track lines enclosed by a single returning arrow bending down the right side and pointing left at the bottom. Preserve the simple readable silhouette, do not turn it into a letter, do not introduce musical notes or Spotify wave marks. Replace the flat orange tile with a premium three-dimensional liquid-glass squircle, viewed straight on and optically centered. Spotify palette: luminous emerald green #1ED760, near black #121212, and white specular reflections. The tile is thick clear green-tinted glass with rounded corners, polished beveled edges, subtle translucent depth and realistic refraction. The original history arrow and horizontal lines are a clear opaque white inset with a very slight extruded depth, immediately readable at 32px and 16px. Controlled studio lighting, bright crisp upper-left rim and soft dark right bevel; restrained, beautiful, tactile, sophisticated. The glass tile occupies the centered 75% of the canvas, leaving 12.5% truly transparent padding on each side for the Chrome store icon. No floor, no background plane, no glow outside the tile, no cast shadow outside the tile, no text, no captions, no logos other than the original supplied history symbol. Output one large square icon, no collage, no mockup, no extra objects.

## Mesh backdrop prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: final soft mesh-gradient backdrop for polished Apple-style Chrome store screenshots, landscape 16:10. Design direction: extremely refined diffuse mesh gradient, smooth blurred color fields that blend into one another with no visible shapes. Base near-black #080C0A. Spotify emerald #1ED760 softened into dark jade, pale mint and subtle silver-white light. The left 60% stays mostly near-black and calm for large white typography. On the right and upper-right, introduce spacious soft blooms of green and mint with organic smooth transitions, a restrained silver-green highlight near the lower middle, and fine even film grain to prevent banding. Quiet, contemporary, expensive, like the abstract atmospheric backgrounds in Apple software product photography. Only a two-dimensional atmospheric mesh-gradient background. Absolutely no objects, no glass panels, no glass squares or rectangles, no frames, no waves, no ribbons, no rings, no orbs, no spheres, no sharp boundaries, no floor, no reflections, no UI, no text, no logos, no music symbols. Color should feel luminous and softly blended rather than neon, black should still dominate. Keep it elegant and unobtrusive so the real app screenshot will be the focal point.
